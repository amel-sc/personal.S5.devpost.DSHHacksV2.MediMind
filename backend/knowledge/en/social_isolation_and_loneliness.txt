Social isolation and loneliness

Definition
A feeling of lacking satisfying social connection, which can occur when entering a new institution, country, or city.

Common signs and symptoms
•⁠  ⁠Feeling lonely despite the presence of others
•⁠  ⁠Difficulty initiating or maintaining relationships
•⁠  ⁠Gradually avoiding social activities
•⁠  ⁠Feeling like you don't "belong" to the group

Frequent causes
•⁠  ⁠Change of environment (new city, country, institution)
•⁠  ⁠Shyness, social anxiety
•⁠  ⁠Overloaded schedule leaving little room for relationships
•⁠  ⁠Cultural or language differences

Suggested solutions

Building connections
•⁠  ⁠Join a club, student association, or group with shared interests
•⁠  ⁠Initiate informal moments yourself (meals, group study sessions, short outings)
•⁠  ⁠Attend campus events even without much initial motivation

Maintaining existing connections
•⁠  ⁠Stay in touch with family and friends at a distance through regular calls
•⁠  ⁠Plan visits or reunions when distance allows

Local resources
•⁠  ⁠Identify a resource person on campus (tutor, advisor, international student liaison)
•⁠  ⁠Join groups for students in similar situations (newcomers, exchange students)
