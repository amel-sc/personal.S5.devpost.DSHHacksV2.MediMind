Addictions (screens, substances)

Definition
Excessive and hard-to-control use of a behavior (screens, gaming, social media) or a substance, despite negative consequences on academic or personal life.

Common signs and symptoms
•⁠  ⁠Inability to reduce time spent on an activity despite intending to
•⁠  ⁠Using it as a systematic escape from stress or boredom
•⁠  ⁠Negative impact on sleep, studies, or relationships
•⁠  ⁠Irritability when unable to access the behavior or substance

Frequent causes
•⁠  ⁠Stress, boredom, loneliness
•⁠  ⁠Constant availability of screens and social media
•⁠  ⁠Seeking immediate gratification in the face of academic pressure
•⁠  ⁠Habits formed gradually without awareness

Suggested solutions

Gradual reduction
•⁠  ⁠Set screen-free time blocks, especially before bedtime
•⁠  ⁠Gradually replace the behavior with an alternative activity (sports, reading, creativity)
•⁠  ⁠Use screen-time tracking tools to become aware of actual usage

Understanding triggers
•⁠  ⁠Identify the triggers (boredom, stress, loneliness) that drive the behavior
•⁠  ⁠Anticipate these moments with a prepared alternative

Support
•⁠  ⁠Talk about it without judgment to a trusted person
•⁠  ⁠Seek specialized support if the behavior becomes difficult to control alone
