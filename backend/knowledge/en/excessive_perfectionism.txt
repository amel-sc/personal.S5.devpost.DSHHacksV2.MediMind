Excessive perfectionism

Definition
Setting unrealistic standards for oneself, with very low tolerance for error or imperfection.

Common signs and symptoms
•⁠  ⁠Chronic dissatisfaction despite good results
•⁠  ⁠Excessive time spent on details at the expense of the big picture
•⁠  ⁠Fear of submitting "imperfect" work
•⁠  ⁠Procrastination linked to fear of not reaching the desired standard

Frequent causes
•⁠  ⁠Very high family or personal expectations
•⁠  ⁠Self-esteem conditioned on performance
•⁠  ⁠Fear of judgment or disappointing others

Suggested solutions

Redefining goals
•⁠  ⁠Set realistic, measurable standards rather than absolute ones
•⁠  ⁠Define in advance a "good enough" threshold for each task
•⁠  ⁠Accept submitting work that is "well done" rather than "perfect"

Working on thought patterns
•⁠  ⁠Identify "all or nothing" thinking and nuance it
•⁠  ⁠Reframe failure or mistakes as a normal part of learning
•⁠  ⁠Practice self-compassion in the face of imperfection

Experimentation
•⁠  ⁠Deliberately practice submitting "imperfect" work to reduce the associated fear
•⁠  ⁠Seek outside feedback to get an objective view of the work's actual quality
