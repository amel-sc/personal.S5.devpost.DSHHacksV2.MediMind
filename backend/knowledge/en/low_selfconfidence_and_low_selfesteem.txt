Low self-confidence and low self-esteem

Definition
A negative perception of one's own worth or abilities, which can affect motivation and willingness to take academic risks.

Common signs and symptoms
•⁠  ⁠Frequent and harsh self-criticism
•⁠  ⁠Difficulty accepting compliments
•⁠  ⁠Avoiding situations where one might be judged
•⁠  ⁠Constant comparison with other students
•⁠  ⁠Excessive need for external validation

Frequent causes
•⁠  ⁠Repeated experiences of failure or criticism
•⁠  ⁠Social comparison, particularly via social media
•⁠  ⁠Very high personal or family expectations
•⁠  ⁠Lack of recognition for past achievements

Suggested solutions

Self-recognition
•⁠  ⁠Regularly list your achievements, even small ones (a "wins" notebook)
•⁠  ⁠Set progressive goals and celebrate each milestone reached
•⁠  ⁠Identify your personal strengths independent of academic results

Changing perspective
•⁠  ⁠Work on assertiveness through gradual practice situations
•⁠  ⁠Replace self-criticism with a kinder inner voice
•⁠  ⁠Avoid constant comparison with other students

Environment
•⁠  ⁠Surround yourself with supportive, encouraging people
•⁠  ⁠Limit exposure to content that generates negative comparison
•⁠  ⁠Ask for constructive feedback from trusted people rather than judging yourself alone
