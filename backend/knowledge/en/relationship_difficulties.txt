Relationship difficulties (friends, family, partner)

Definition
Tensions or conflicts in interpersonal relationships that can affect well-being and academic concentration.

Common signs and symptoms
•⁠  ⁠Repeated conflicts with loved ones, friends, or a partner
•⁠  ⁠Feeling misunderstood or relationally lonely
•⁠  ⁠Difficulty expressing needs or boundaries
•⁠  ⁠Relationship tension affecting sleep or concentration

Frequent causes
•⁠  ⁠Change in lifestyle and priorities when starting studies
•⁠  ⁠Difficulty balancing academic and relational life
•⁠  ⁠Different communication styles
•⁠  ⁠Emotional dependence or fear of conflict

Suggested solutions

Communication
•⁠  ⁠Practice clear, respectful communication of everyone's needs
•⁠  ⁠Use "I" statements rather than accusations ("I feel..." rather than "you always...")
•⁠  ⁠Take a step back before reacting in the heat of a conflict

Boundaries and autonomy
•⁠  ⁠Learn to set boundaries without feeling guilty
•⁠  ⁠Cultivate relationships based on mutual respect rather than emotional dependence
•⁠  ⁠Preserve time for yourself alongside relationships

Conflict resolution
•⁠  ⁠Seek a neutral third party (mediator, counselor, listening service) if conflict persists
•⁠  ⁠Distinguish occasional disagreements from recurring problematic relationship patterns
