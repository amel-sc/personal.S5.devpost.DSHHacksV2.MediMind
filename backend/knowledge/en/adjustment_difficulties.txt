Adjustment difficulties (new environment, distance from family, mobility)

Definition
Emotional and behavioral difficulties related to a major change in life circumstances (moving, a new institution, a new country).

Common signs and symptoms
•⁠  ⁠Feeling a loss of reference points
•⁠  ⁠Intense homesickness for the place or people left behind
•⁠  ⁠Difficulty engaging with the new environment
•⁠  ⁠Anxiety about cultural or administrative differences

Frequent causes
•⁠  ⁠Abrupt change of living environment
•⁠  ⁠Distance from one's usual support network
•⁠  ⁠Cultural, linguistic, or administrative differences
•⁠  ⁠Lack of preparation before leaving

Suggested solutions

Reference points and transition
•⁠  ⁠Keep familiar reference points (personal objects, habits, photos)
•⁠  ⁠Gradually explore the new environment rather than everything at once
•⁠  ⁠Give yourself time to adjust without excessive pressure

Staying connected to home
•⁠  ⁠Keep up an activity or hobby practiced before arriving in the new place
•⁠  ⁠Maintain regular contact with loved ones left behind

Integration
•⁠  ⁠Join groups of students in the same situation (newcomers, exchange program)
•⁠  ⁠Learn about administrative and cultural procedures in advance to reduce uncertainty
