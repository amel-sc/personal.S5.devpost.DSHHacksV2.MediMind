Eating disorders and relationship with food

Definition
Disturbances in eating behavior (restriction, binges, excessive concern about weight) that may appear or worsen during periods of student stress.

Common signs and symptoms
•⁠  ⁠Significant food restriction or repeatedly skipping meals
•⁠  ⁠Binge eating episodes or loss of control over eating
•⁠  ⁠Excessive concern about weight or appearance
•⁠  ⁠Frequent weighing, constant comparison with others
•⁠  ⁠Avoidance of social meals

Frequent causes
•⁠  ⁠Stress, anxiety, need for control
•⁠  ⁠Social or aesthetic pressure
•⁠  ⁠Disorganized student lifestyle
•⁠  ⁠Isolation or underlying emotional difficulties

Suggested solutions

Eating behavior
•⁠  ⁠Favor regular, varied meals without rigid food rules
•⁠  ⁠Eat mindfully, without distractions (screens, studying)
•⁠  ⁠Prepare meals in advance to avoid skipping them due to lack of time

Relationship with body image
•⁠  ⁠Favor a calmer relationship with food rather than strict rules
•⁠  ⁠Avoid weighing yourself or comparing yourself to others frequently
•⁠  ⁠Limit exposure to appearance-focused content if it generates anxiety

Support
•⁠  ⁠Talk openly to a trusted person if in doubt or difficulty
•⁠  ⁠Seek a specialized professional (doctor, dietitian, psychologist) if concerning signs appear
•⁠  ⁠Don't minimize early signs, even subtle ones
