Social anxiety

Definition
An intense and persistent fear of social or performance situations, linked to the fear of others' judgment.

Common signs and symptoms
•⁠  ⁠Marked fear of public speaking or participating in class
•⁠  ⁠Avoiding group work or oral presentations
•⁠  ⁠Physical symptoms (blushing, trembling, sweating) in social situations
•⁠  ⁠Excessive rumination after a social interaction

Frequent causes
•⁠  ⁠Past negative experiences of judgment or ridicule
•⁠  ⁠Lack of gradual exposure to social situations
•⁠  ⁠Perfectionism or intense fear of making mistakes in public

Suggested solutions

Gradual exposure
•⁠  ⁠Gradually expose yourself to social situations of increasing difficulty
•⁠  ⁠Start with small groups before large presentations
•⁠  ⁠Prepare and rehearse oral presentations in advance

Coping techniques
•⁠  ⁠Practice slow breathing before speaking
•⁠  ⁠Refocus attention on the content of the message rather than perceived judgment
•⁠  ⁠Accept that a certain level of nervousness is normal and often less visible than it feels

Support
•⁠  ⁠Participate in public speaking or assertiveness workshops if offered by the institution
•⁠  ⁠Talk to an academic advisor about accommodations if needed (e.g., presentations in small groups)
