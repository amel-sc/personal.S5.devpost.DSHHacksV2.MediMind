Fear of failure

Definition
Intense dread of the consequences of failure, which can lead to avoidance, procrastination, or disproportionate stress.

Common signs and symptoms
•⁠  ⁠Avoiding situations with a risk of failure (choosing easy topics, giving up early)
•⁠  ⁠Disproportionate anxiety before an assessment
•⁠  ⁠Excessive need for control over outcomes
•⁠  ⁠Difficulty recovering from a past failure

Frequent causes
•⁠  ⁠Past experiences of failure perceived as very negative
•⁠  ⁠Family or social pressure to succeed
•⁠  ⁠Self-esteem strongly tied to academic results

Suggested solutions

Changing perspective
•⁠  ⁠Redefine failure as information rather than a judgment of personal worth
•⁠  ⁠Identify examples of success that followed a failure (your own or others')
•⁠  ⁠Separate personal worth from the results obtained

Gradual action
•⁠  ⁠Gradually expose yourself to situations with moderate risk of failure to desensitize the fear
•⁠  ⁠Prepare a realistic "plan B" to reduce all-or-nothing pressure

Support
•⁠  ⁠Share your fears with a loved one or academic advisor
•⁠  ⁠Analyze a past failure factually to draw concrete lessons from it
