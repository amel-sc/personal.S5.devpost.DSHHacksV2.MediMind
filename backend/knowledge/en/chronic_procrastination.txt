Chronic procrastination

Definition
A repeated tendency to postpone important tasks despite being aware of the negative consequences.

Common signs and symptoms
•⁠  ⁠Systematically postponing tasks until the last minute
•⁠  ⁠Guilt or anxiety related to the resulting delay
•⁠  ⁠Easily distracted (social media, entertainment)
•⁠  ⁠Difficulty starting a task even a simple one

Frequent causes
•⁠  ⁠Fear of failure or perfectionism
•⁠  ⁠A task perceived as too large or unclear
•⁠  ⁠Lack of intrinsic motivation
•⁠  ⁠Decision fatigue or mental overload

Suggested solutions

Getting started
•⁠  ⁠Break tasks into very concrete, short actions ("open the document," "write one paragraph")
•⁠  ⁠Start with the simplest task to build momentum (the 2-minute rule)
•⁠  ⁠Set a very short time to begin ("just 10 minutes")

Work environment
•⁠  ⁠Remove distractions during work sessions (phone on airplane mode, notifications off)
•⁠  ⁠Work in a space dedicated solely to work (library, desk)

Structure and commitment
•⁠  ⁠Set intermediate deadlines, not just the final one
•⁠  ⁠Work with a peer or group to create social accountability
•⁠  ⁠Reward the completion of each step
