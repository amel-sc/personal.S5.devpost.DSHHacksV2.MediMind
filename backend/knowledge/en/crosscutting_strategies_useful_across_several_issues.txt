Cross-cutting strategies useful across several issues

•⁠  ⁠Keep a regular journal to identify recurring emotional patterns
•⁠  ⁠Engage in regular physical activity, beneficial for stress, sleep, and mood
•⁠  ⁠Maintain a supportive social network (friends, family, peers, associations)
•⁠  ⁠Learn and practice simple breathing and relaxation techniques
•⁠  ⁠Structure your time with organizational tools (calendar, lists, time blocks)
•⁠  ⁠Normalize seeking psychological or academic support when needed
•⁠  ⁠Avoid facing difficulties in isolation; talk about them early rather than waiting for them to worsen
•⁠  ⁠Identify the services available within your institution (health service, social service, academic advisors, student associations)
