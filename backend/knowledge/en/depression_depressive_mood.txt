Depression / depressive mood

Definition
An emotional state marked by persistent sadness, loss of interest or pleasure, and decreased energy, lasting several weeks.

Common signs and symptoms
•⁠  ⁠Sadness or emotional emptiness almost daily
•⁠  ⁠Loss of interest in previously enjoyable activities
•⁠  ⁠Fatigue, lack of energy, slowed-down feeling
•⁠  ⁠Sleep disturbances (too much or too little) and appetite changes
•⁠  ⁠Difficulty concentrating, feelings of worthlessness
•⁠  ⁠Progressive isolation

Frequent causes
•⁠  ⁠Accumulation of academic and personal stress
•⁠  ⁠Social isolation, being away from family
•⁠  ⁠Difficult life events (breakup, failure, grief)
•⁠  ⁠Lack of meaning or perspective in one's studies

Suggested solutions

Routine and structure
•⁠  ⁠Maintain a stable daily routine (wake-up time, meals, bedtime)
•⁠  ⁠Set very small, achievable goals each day (getting up, going outside for 10 minutes)
•⁠  ⁠Structure the day even without initial motivation

Behavioral activation
•⁠  ⁠Gradually resume enjoyable activities, even briefly
•⁠  ⁠Get exposure to natural light and go outside at least once a day
•⁠  ⁠Engage in gentle physical activity (walking, stretching)

Social connection
•⁠  ⁠Stay in regular contact with loved ones, even through short messages
•⁠  ⁠Avoid complete isolation, even when the desire to socialize is low
•⁠  ⁠Identify a trusted person to talk to about how you're feeling

Monitoring
•⁠  ⁠Track your mood over several weeks (a simple journal)
•⁠  ⁠Consult a mental health professional if sadness persists for more than two weeks or worsens
•⁠  ⁠Contact a listening service or the institution's student health service
