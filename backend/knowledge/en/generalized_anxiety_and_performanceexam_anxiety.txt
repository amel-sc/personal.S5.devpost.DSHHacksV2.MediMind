Generalized anxiety and performance/exam anxiety

Definition
Excessive and persistent worry about results, the future, or performance, which can intensify before assessments.

Common signs and symptoms
•⁠  ⁠Mental rumination, catastrophic scenarios
•⁠  ⁠Racing heart, sweaty palms, trembling before an exam
•⁠  ⁠Memory blanks or freezing during an assessment despite good preparation
•⁠  ⁠Avoidance of evaluative situations (postponements, absences)
•⁠  ⁠Excessive need for reassurance

Frequent causes
•⁠  ⁠Pressure to succeed (family, personal, social)
•⁠  ⁠Past experiences of failure or negative judgment
•⁠  ⁠Lack of preparation, or conversely, excessive perfectionism
•⁠  ⁠Fear of others' judgment

Suggested solutions

Concrete preparation
•⁠  ⁠Prepare through mock exams under conditions close to the real one
•⁠  ⁠Revise in short, regular sessions rather than all at once (spaced repetition)
•⁠  ⁠Prepare a checklist of materials and administrative steps to reduce uncertainty
•⁠  ⁠Arrive early on the day of the test to avoid extra stress

Regulation techniques
•⁠  ⁠Practice slow abdominal breathing (inhale 4s, exhale 6s)
•⁠  ⁠Use the "grounding" technique (5-4-3-2-1 senses) during a spike of anxiety
•⁠  ⁠Keep a journal to externalize worries before sleeping
•⁠  ⁠Identify automatic anxious thoughts and challenge them with concrete facts

Lifestyle
•⁠  ⁠Limit caffeine and stimulants before stressful periods
•⁠  ⁠Sleep enough the night before an assessment rather than studying late
•⁠  ⁠Engage in regular physical activity to reduce baseline anxiety levels

Support
•⁠  ⁠Talk about your anxiety with a loved one, a tutor, or a student support service
•⁠  ⁠Participate in stress-management workshops offered by the institution if available
