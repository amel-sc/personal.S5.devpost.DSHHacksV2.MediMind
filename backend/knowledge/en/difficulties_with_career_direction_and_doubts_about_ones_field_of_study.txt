Difficulties with career direction and doubts about one's field of study

Definition
Uncertainty or questioning about one's choice of program, career, or professional project, a source of anxiety and demotivation.

Common signs and symptoms
•⁠  ⁠Feeling out of place in one's current program
•⁠  ⁠Progressive loss of motivation or interest in coursework
•⁠  ⁠Anxious comparison with peers who seem to have a clearer plan
•⁠  ⁠Repeated doubt about the relevance of continuing the current path

Frequent causes
•⁠  ⁠A choice of program driven mainly by family or social pressure
•⁠  ⁠Lack of information about the field's actual career prospects
•⁠  ⁠Natural evolution of personal interests over time

Suggested solutions

Clarification
•⁠  ⁠Take stock of your values, interests, and skills independent of outside expectations
•⁠  ⁠Seek out testimonials or meet professionals in fields of interest
•⁠  ⁠Explore internships, projects, or short experiences to test a field before changing direction

Guidance
•⁠  ⁠Consult a career counselor or student support service
•⁠  ⁠Discuss your doubts with teachers or former students from the same program

Gradual decision-making
•⁠  ⁠Consider gradual adjustments (an elective, a specialization) before a full change of direction
•⁠  ⁠Remember that changing paths is not a failure but an adjustment
